# Changelog

Released cheat-sheet sets are versioned under `dist/`. Each entry notes what
changed so older printed copies can be traced back to their source.

## v1.0 — 2026-07-24

First complete set. Four A4 quick-reference sheets, one per Jaws of the Lion
mercenary, in the "Card Frame" design (design/variations/variation-1).

- **Sheet I · Scenario Setup** (Hatchet) — setup checklist, opening-a-door
  timing, scenario-level numbers, common setup traps.
- **Sheet II · The Round** (Red Guard) — round sequence, end-of-round upkeep,
  elements, conditions, monster focus, taking damage, movement (Jump/Fly).
- **Sheet III · The Reckoning** (Voidwarden) — scenario end, XP & gold,
  battle goals, scenario-level table, town steps, failure handling.
- **Sheet IV · Levelling Up** (Demolitionist) — level-up steps, XP thresholds,
  perks & battle-goal checks, milestones, scenario difficulty.

All rules verified against the official JOTL rulebook. Content source of truth
is `content/CONTENT.md`.
