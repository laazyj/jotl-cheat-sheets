Scenario Setup

BEFORE THE FIRST CARD IS PLAYED
HATCHET
Setup Checklist
  1. Read the intro in the scenario book — and the Special Rules box now. It often changes setup: spawns, round counting, allies.
  2. Set scenario level: average party level ÷ 2, rounded up. Easy −1 · Normal +0 · Hard +1 · Very Hard +2.
  3. !Build the first map section ONLY. Anything drawn in a later section — monsters included — is not placed now. It enters when that section is revealed.
  4. Place monsters for YOUR player count — only standees whose 2/3/4-player icon matches. Gold-ringed hex = elite. Slide each stat card to the scenario level.
  5. Monster ability decks: one shuffled deck per monster type in the scenario — including types arriving later.
  6. !Battle goals: deal 2 to each player. Secretly keep 1, discard the other.
  7. Attack modifier decks: your 20 base cards + your perk changes, plus the monster deck. Strip out stale bless/curse from last session. Shuffle all.
  8. Elements board: all six markers to Inert. Round tracker to 1 if the scenario counts rounds.
  9. Characters: HP dial to max · pick your hand after reading the scenario · items readied & unflipped.
  10. Place figures on the starting hexes — then draw the first cards.
Choosing Your Hand
  • Hand picked after reading the scenario — tune for objective, room count and monster types.
  • Long scenario? Ration Lost cards — each one burnt is a shorter fuse on exhaustion.
  • Items may be swapped freely before the scenario, never during.
Opening a Door
  • Reveal the section; place overlays and its monsters — your player count only, again.
  • If that type has no ability card flipped yet this round, flip one now. Revealed monsters always act this round.
  • !Initiative lower than the opener (already passed)? They act immediately after that character's turn. Otherwise at their normal slot.
  • Opening the door is part of the move — finish any remaining movement after.
  • Doors stay open. Revealed stays revealed.

Traps We Fall Into
  • Battle goal cards never dealt.
  • Whole map populated at setup — monster flood.
  • Perk cards missing from the modifier deck.
  • Stat sleeves still on last scenario's level.
  • Special-rules box discovered at round six.
Oft Forgot
  • You may forego any attack, push or pull — but a used push/pull goes to full effect.
  • Summons don't act the round they arrive, and never drop loot.
  • Your deck is your clock — burn Lost cards early, exhaust early.

The Round

KEEP THE WHEEL TURNING
RED GUARD
Round Sequence
  1. SELECT — everyone secretly picks 2 cards, or declares Long Rest (needs ≥2 cards in discard). Can do neither → exhausted.
  2. REVEAL — flip one ability card per monster type on the map.
  3. INITIATIVE — your leading card sets your number; monsters use their card. Lowest first. Char/monster tie → character; char/char tie → second cards.
  4. TURNS — top of one card + bottom of the other, either order. Either half may become the default Attack 2 (top) or Move 2 (bottom) — still counts as playing it.
  5. END OF ROUND — see the box below.
End of Round
  • !Elements wane one step: Strong → Waning → Inert.
  • !Reshuffle any modifier deck that drew a null (∅) or ×2 this round. Bless & Curse do NOT trigger a reshuffle — they're removed from the deck when drawn.
  • Reshuffle a monster ability deck if its shuffle-icon card came up.
  • Short rest (optional, once): shuffle discard, lose 1 at random, rest back. May pay 1 HP to re-roll the loss, once.
Conditions
  • Stun · Immobilize · Disarm · Muddle · Strengthen · Invisible: last until the end of the target's NEXT turn — not end of round.
  • Poison: +1 damage from every attack; any heal removes it instead of healing.
  • Wound: 1 damage at turn start; any heal removes it (heal still applies).
Monster Turns
  • !Focus = the character reachable by the shortest path (not straight-line). Monsters route around obstacles and treat traps as obstacles — the nearest-looking hero isn't always the target. No line-of-sight needed.
  • Same number of hexes → focus the one earlier in initiative.
  • Within a type: elites act first, then normals, ascending standee number.
  • The ability card is the whole turn — no attack listed, no attack.
Elements
  • An element you create is usable only at the end of that turn — never fuel your own combo the same turn.
  • Consuming drops it to Inert at once; only one figure gets it.
Taking Damage
  • You may ALWAYS instead lose 1 card from hand or 2 from discard to negate ALL damage from one source.
  • Long rest (init 99, whole turn): recover discards (lose 1 of choice), heal 2, refresh all spent items.
Moving Around
  • Difficult terrain: costs 2 movement to enter each hex.
  • !Jump: ignores figures, difficult terrain, traps & hazards crossed — only the landing hex must be legal & empty.
  • Fly: like Jump, but also ignores obstacles crossed en route.
  • Move through allies freely, never enemies; never end on another figure.

The Reckoning

SCENARIO END & SPOILS
VOIDWARDEN
When Does It End?
  • !Goal met → the scenario ends at the end of that round — keep playing it out: loot, finish battle-goal business.
  • Loss = everyone exhausted, or the goal becomes impossible.
  • Coins left on the floor vanish — nobody banks them.
Everyone — Win or Lose
  • Bank the XP tracked on your dial/cards during the scenario.
  • Convert looted coin tokens to gold at the scenario-level rate (see table). Exhausted characters keep what they gathered before dropping.
On Victory
  • !Bonus XP = 4 + (2 × scenario level) each — including exhausted characters. (Level 1 = 6.)
  • Reveal battle goals: fulfilled → mark the ✓ shown (1 or 2). Every 3rd ✓ = 1 perk (max 18 ✓).
  • Tick the scenario complete; read the conclusion; apply unlocks & rewards; note the next scenario(s).

Back in Town
  • Refresh & unflip all items; clear conditions; reset HP; all cards return to hand.
  • Shop: buy at cost, sell at half (round down).
  • Level up if XP allows (see Sheet IV).
  • Encounter one city event after every successful scenario (Scenario 3 on): draw, pick one of two options together, file it away.
On Failure
  • Money tokens, XP & treasure loot earned before failure carry into the retry. Everything else resets. (Treasure loots once, ever.)
  • No completion bonus. !Battle goals score NOTHING on a loss — the ✓ only lands on a completed scenario. Card is discarded either way; draw fresh next time.
  • Set the scenario up again and replay at no penalty.

Levelling Up

BACK AT THE TABLE
DEMOLITIONIST
Level-Up Steps
  1. Tick the new level on your character sheet. You never lose XP.
  2. !Add ONE new ability card to your pool: any card of your new level or lower you don't own. Just one — not both of the level's cards.
  3. !Hand size never changes. Bigger pool, same hand.
  4. Raise max HP to the white number below the new level on your character mat.
  5. !Gain 1 perk — apply it to the modifier deck NOW (add/remove the physical cards) or it never happens.
  6. Recount the party scenario level (avg level ÷ 2, round up) — monsters may level with you.

Scenario Difficulty
  • Normal = party level ÷ 2, round up. Easy −1 · Hard +1 · Very Hard +2.
  • Only raise the scenario (monster) level once someone reaches level 3.
Perks & Battle Goals
  • Perks are the ONLY way to improve your modifier deck — remove the base cards you're told to and add the perk cards.
  • Every 3rd battle-goal ✓ = 1 extra perk (max 18 ✓ = 6 perks). Apply it the same way, right away.
  • Perks that "remove" cards: pull them and store them — mind overlaps if two perks touch the same card.
Milestones
  • Level 3: scenario level ticks up, and you may carry small items = half your level, rounded up.
  • Level 5: open your second ability pack — higher-level cards plus special instructions.
Worth Remembering
  • Levelling grants no new gold, items or hand cards — only the card, HP and perk above.
  • JOTL has no personal quests or retirement — you keep your mercenary the whole campaign.
