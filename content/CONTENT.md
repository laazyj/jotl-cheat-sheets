# JOTL Cheat Sheets — Proposed Content (v1 for review)

Four A4 sheets, one per trouble area, each themed to one of the four mercenaries.
Items marked ⚑ are the "we always forget this" callouts that get visual emphasis on
the sheet. Items marked (?) are ones I recommend you verify against your rulebook /
glossary before we lock the content — they're right to the best of my research, but
JOTL occasionally diverges from Gloomhaven classic in small ways.

---

## Sheet 1 — SCENARIO SETUP · "Before the First Card" (Hatchet)

### Setup checklist (in order)

1. **Read the intro** in the scenario book. Read any **Special Rules** box now —
   it often changes setup (spawns, round tracking, allies).
2. **Set the scenario level**: average party level ÷ 2, **rounded up**.
   Easy −1 · Normal +0 · Hard +1 · Very Hard +2.
3. **Build the map — first section ONLY.** ⚑ Anything shown in a section
   revealed later (door icons, lettered/numbered sections) is NOT placed now,
   monsters included. If a monster stands in a later section, it enters later.
4. **Place monsters for YOUR player count.** Only standees whose 2/3/4-player
   icon matches your party size. Gold-ringed hex = elite. Set each monster's
   stat card sleeve to the scenario level.
5. **Monster ability decks**: one shuffled deck per monster *type* on the map
   (including types that appear later — have them ready).
6. **Battle goals** ⚑: deal 2 to each player; each secretly keeps 1, discards 1.
7. **Attack modifier decks**: your own 20-card deck **plus your perk changes**,
   and the monster deck. Strip out any bless/curse still in there from last
   session. Shuffle everything.
8. **Elements board**: all six to INERT. Round tracker to 1 if the scenario
   counts rounds.
9. **Characters**: HP dial to max for your level · choose your hand *after*
   reading the scenario · items readied (unspent/unflipped).
10. **Place figures** on the starting hexes.

### Opening a door (mid-scenario "setup")

- Reveal the new section; place its overlays and its monsters **for your player
  count** only.
- If that monster type has **no ability card flipped yet this round**, flip one
  now. Newly-revealed monsters **always act this round**.
- ⚑ If their initiative is **lower** than the character who opened the door (it
  already passed), they act **immediately after that character's turn**.
  Otherwise they act at their normal initiative slot.
- Opening the door is part of the move — finish your remaining movement after.
- Doors stay open. Revealed = revealed.

### Setup traps we've fallen into (call-out strip)

- ⚑ Battle goal cards not dealt.
- ⚑ Whole map populated at setup → monster flood.
- Perk cards not in the modifier deck (deck still base-20).
- Stat sleeves left on last scenario's level.
- Special-rules box discovered halfway through the scenario.

## Sheet 2 — THE ROUND · "Keep the Wheel Turning" (Red Guard)

### Round sequence

1. **SELECT** — everyone secretly picks 2 cards (or declares **Long Rest** —
   needs ≥2 cards in discard). Can't do either → **exhausted**.
2. **REVEAL** — flip one ability card per monster type on the map.
3. **INITIATIVE** — leading card sets your number; monsters use their ability
   card. Lowest acts first. **Character/monster tie → character first**;
   character/character tie → compare **second cards**.
4. **TURNS** — on your turn: **top of one card + bottom of the other**, either
   order. Either half may be swapped for the default: **top → Attack 2,
   bottom → Move 2** (still counts as playing the card).
5. **END OF ROUND** — the bit we forget, see below.

### End of round ⚑

- **Elements wane one step**: Strong → Waning → Inert. ⚑
- **Reshuffle any attack-modifier deck that showed a ∅ (null) or ×2 this
  round** (the shuffle-icon cards). ⚑ Bless & Curse do NOT trigger a
  reshuffle — they are **removed from the deck entirely when drawn**.
- **Reshuffle a monster ability deck** if its shuffle-icon card came up.
- **Short rest** (optional, once per round-end): shuffle your discard, lose 1 at
  random, take the rest back to hand. May pay 1 HP to re-randomise the loss, once.

### Elements — the two rules

- An element you **create** becomes available at the **end of that turn** —
  never fuel your own combo in the same turn.
- **Consuming** drops it to Inert immediately; only one figure gets it.

### Conditions

- Stun · Immobilize · Disarm · Muddle · Strengthen · Invisible: last until the
  **end of the target's NEXT turn** — not the end of the round.
- Poison: +1 damage from every attack; **any heal removes it instead of healing**.
- Wound: 1 damage at turn start; any heal removes it (heal still applies).

### Monster turns (box)

- Focus = the character reachable by the **shortest path** (not straight-line
  distance). Monsters route **around obstacles** and treat **traps as obstacles**
  — they won't cross a trap to reach you unless it's the only route. ⚑ So the
  nearest-looking hero isn't always the target. No line-of-sight needed.
- Reachable in the **same** number of hexes → focus the one **earlier in
  initiative order**.
- Within a type: **elites act first**, then normals, each in ascending standee
  number.
- The ability card is the whole turn — no attack listed, no attack.

### When you take damage (box)

- You may ALWAYS instead lose **1 card from hand or 2 from discard** to negate
  ALL damage from one source.
- **Long rest**: initiative 99, full turn: recover discards (choose 1 to lose),
  **heal 2, refresh all spent items**.

### Moving around (box)

- **Difficult terrain**: costs **2 movement** to enter each hex.
- ⚑ **Jump**: ignores figures, difficult terrain, traps & hazards *passed over* —
  only the **landing hex** must be a legal, empty space.
- **Fly**: like Jump, but also ignores obstacles crossed en route.
- You may move **through allies** freely, never through enemies, and can never
  **end** on another figure's hex.

## Sheet 3 — THE RECKONING · "Scenario End & Spoils" (Voidwarden)

### When does it actually end?

- Goal met → the scenario ends at the **end of that round** ⚑ — keep playing
  the round out: loot, finish battle-goal business.
- Loss = everyone exhausted, or the goal becomes impossible.
- Coins left on the floor **vanish** — nobody banks them.

### Everyone, win or lose

- Bank the **XP tracked on your dial/cards** during the scenario.
- Convert **looted coin tokens** to gold at the scenario-level rate (table
  below). Exhausted characters keep what they gathered before dropping.

### On VICTORY only

- **Bonus XP = 4 + (2 × scenario level)** each — including characters who were
  exhausted during the scenario (a completed scenario rewards *every*
  character; at the default level 1 that's 6).
- Reveal **battle goals**: fulfilled → mark the ✓ shown (1 or 2).
  **Every 3rd ✓ = 1 extra perk.**
- Tick the scenario complete; read the conclusion; apply unlocks/rewards; note
  the next scenario(s).

### Scenario-level table

| Scenario level | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monster level  | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
| Gold per coin  | 1 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 |
| Trap damage    | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
| Victory bonus XP | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 18 |

### Back in town

- Refresh/unflip **all items**; clear conditions; reset HP; all cards back.
- Shop: buy at cost, **sell at half** (round down).
- Level up if XP allows (see Sheet 4).
- **Encounter one city event after every successful scenario** (from Scenario 3
  onward): draw the top event card, pick one of its two options together, then
  file it on the "Encountered Events" side. Apply any conclusion reward / item
  unlock the scenario granted.

### On failure

- **Money tokens, XP, and treasure-tile loot earned before failure carry into
  the retry.** Everything else resets. (Treasure tiles loot only once, ever.)
- No completion bonus XP. ⚑ **Battle goals score NOTHING on a loss** — the check
  mark only lands if the scenario is *completed*, even if you met the goal's
  condition. The card is discarded win or lose; draw a fresh one next scenario.
- The scenario is set up again from the start and replayed at no penalty.

## Sheet 4 — LEVELLING UP · "Back at the Table" (Demolitionist)

### XP thresholds

| Level | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| XP    | 45 | 95 | 150 | 210 | 275 | 345 | 420 | 500 |

### Level-up steps (in town, in order)

1. **Tick the new level** on your character sheet.
2. **Add ONE new ability card** to your pool: any card of your **new level or
   lower** you don't already own. One. Not both of the level's cards. ⚑
3. **Hand size does not change.** Ever. Bigger pool, same hand. ⚑
4. **Raise max HP** to the new level's value on your character mat's table.
5. **Gain 1 perk** — apply it to your attack modifier deck NOW (add/remove the
   physical cards) or it never happens. ⚑
6. Recount the **party's scenario level** (avg level ÷ 2, round up) — monsters
   may level with you next scenario.

### Also worth remembering

- Battle-goal checkmarks: every 3rd ✓ grants an **extra perk** — same
  procedure, do it while you're at it.
- No new gold, items or hand cards from levelling.
- JOTL has no personal quests / retirement — you keep your mercenary. 
- Perks that "remove" cards: take them out and store them; don't stack removals
  wrongly if two perks touch the same card type.

## Researched "oft-forgot" extras (distribute as small footer strips)

These came out of community threads/articles on commonly-misplayed rules — I've
slotted most into the sheets above; the rest could form a footer strip on each
sheet:

- You can always forego an attack/push/pull entirely, but a push/pull you do
  use must be at full effect where possible.
- Advantage: draw 2, use the better; Disadvantage: draw 2, use the worse.
- Summoned monsters don't act the round they arrive and never drop loot.
- Jump/Fly ignore hexes en route (traps, hazards) — only the landing hex counts.
- Difficult terrain costs 2 move per hex; Jump/Fly ignore it.
- Line of sight is corner-to-corner; doors block until opened.
- Retaliate only triggers if the retaliator survives the hit.
- Shield reduces attack damage only (not trap/hazard damage).
- Invisible characters are treated as obstacles by monsters.
- Your deck is your clock: burn Lost cards early, exhaust early.
